## Note-writing style (mandatory)

- Write content as **markdown bullet lists**: each point on its own line starting with `- `. Never cram multiple ideas into one comma-separated sentence or an inline run.
- Do NOT write paragraph blocks — they are hard to digest. Keep every note scannable: bullets, short lines.
- Use headings, **tables** for anything tabular, callouts (`> [!INFO]` etc.), wikilinks, and embeds alongside the bullets.
- Bold the keyword at the start of each bullet.
- Keep notes concise, high-density, exam-oriented. Group related topics into one note; interlink related notes with wikilinks.
- Frontmatter convention: `Title:` (quoted), `Status:`, `tags: [subject, unit-N]`, `Date: "YYYY.MM.DD"`. No `marker:` link in lecture notes; empty `marker:` allowed in index/policy notes to match originals.
