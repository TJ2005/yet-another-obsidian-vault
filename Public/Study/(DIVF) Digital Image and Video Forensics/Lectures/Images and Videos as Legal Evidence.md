# Images and Videos as Legal Evidence

> [!INFO] Unit 1
> Visual media is increasingly accepted as legal evidence — but admissibility depends on **relevance, authenticity and potential for prejudice**. Courts ask whether the material accurately represents the situation and whether its probative value outweighs its potential negative impact.

## Admissibility factors

| Factor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Relevance** | The image/video must be pertinent to the case — proving or disproving a disputed fact |
| **Authenticity** | The evidence must be shown to be what it purports to be — not manipulated or tampered with |
| **Prejudice** | Overly graphic or sensational material that sways the jury emotionally rather than logically may be excluded |

### Specific considerations

- **Digital evidence** — primary evidence in many jurisdictions, but authenticity and integrity must be carefully verified
- **Chain of custody** — a clear record from collection to courtroom presentation demonstrates integrity
- **Enhancement** — poor-quality media can be enhanced, but alterations must be documented and originals preserved
- **Social media** — platform content is admissible if relevant

## Principles of forensic analysis

| Principle | Application |
| --- | --- |
| **Securing the crime scene** | Prevent unauthorised access, document all processes, disconnect wireless connections |
| **Maintaining chain of custody** | Detailed record of collection, handling and analysis |
| **Preserving evidence integrity** | Work on a **bit-for-bit copy** (forensic image); original stays unaltered |
| **Repeatability & reproducibility** | Same results obtainable by different investigators with same data, tools, techniques |
| **Locard's Exchange Principle** | Every interaction with a computer system leaves a trace — links suspects to actions and devices |
| **Reliable & accurate analysis** | Validated tools and techniques; clear, concise, accurate presentation |

## Analysis techniques

- **Metadata analysis** — date, time, camera settings, software used for creation/modification
- **Steganography detection** — hidden data inside images and videos
- **Image/video enhancement** — brightness, contrast, colour adjustments to reveal detail obscured by noise or compression
- **Video stabilisation** — reducing camera movement for clearer playback
- **Restoration & reconstruction** — recovering damaged or corrupted data
- **Hash value verification** — fingerprinting files to prove integrity and authenticity
