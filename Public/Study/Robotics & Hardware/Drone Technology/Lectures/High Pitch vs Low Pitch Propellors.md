## **Low-Pitch Propellers**
Low-pitch propellers have a **shallower blade angle**, moving less air per revolution. They emphasize **efficiency, endurance, and smoother flight control** rather than speed or thrust.

---

### **1. Improved Efficiency**

* Convert motor power into thrust more efficiently.
* Require **less torque and current**, leading to **longer flight times**.
* Ideal for **survey, mapping, and photography drones** where endurance is prioritized.

$$ \eta = \frac{T \cdot v}{P_{\text{input}}} $$
Efficiency ($\eta$) increases when thrust ($T$) is achieved with less input power.

---

### **2. Smooth Flight**

* Produce **less vibration and turbulence**, ensuring smoother motion.
* Suitable for **aerial photography**, **videography**, or **precision hovering**.
* Offer **better stability** and **less motor strain**.

---

### **3. Reduced Top Speed**

* Lower pitch limits **maximum forward speed** and **acceleration**.
* Better suited for **steady flight** than **aggressive maneuvers**.

---

### **Summary**

| Feature    | Effect    | Best Use                  |
| ---------- | --------- | ------------------------- |
| Thrust     | Moderate  | Photography               |
| Efficiency | High      | Endurance missions        |
| Stability  | Excellent | Survey & Cinematic drones |
| Top Speed  | Limited   | Not for racing            |
