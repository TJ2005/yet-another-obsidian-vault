## 1. Why This Matters

Before studying drone hardware and control systems, you need to understand the **physical principles** that govern how a drone moves. Every maneuver — ascending, hovering, rolling, yawing — has a precise physical and mathematical explanation rooted in Newton's laws and fluid dynamics.

---

## 2. Fundamental Forces on a Drone

A drone in flight is subject to **four forces**:

| Force | Direction | Generated By |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Thrust** (T) | Upward | Spinning propellers pushing air down |
| **Weight** (W) | Downward | Gravitational pull on drone mass |
| **Drag** (D) | Opposing motion | Air resistance on the frame |
| **Lift** (L) | Upward component | Aerodynamic effect of forward motion (fixed-wing) |

For a **quadcopter hover**, drag and lift are negligible. The dominant relationship is simply:

$$T 	ext{ vs } W$$

---

## 3. Vertical Flight States

| Condition | Relation | What Happens |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| **Ascend** | $T > W$ | Net upward force — drone climbs |
| **Hover** | $T = W$ | Net force zero — drone stationary |
| **Descend** | $T < W$ | Net downward force — drone loses altitude |

Applying Newton's second law ($F = ma$):

$$F_{	ext{net}} = T - W = T - mg$$

- When $T > mg$: drone accelerates upward
- When $T = mg$: $a = 0$, constant altitude (hover)
- When $T < mg$: drone accelerates downward

---

## 4. The Three Rotational Axes

A drone has three independent rotational degrees of freedom, each around a body axis:

| Motion | Axis | Symbol | Direction | Visual Effect |
|--------|------|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **Roll** ($\phi$) | Longitudinal (X) | $\phi$ | Tilting left/right | Drone banks sideways |
| **Pitch** ($\theta$) | Lateral (Y) | $\theta$ | Tilting forward/backward | Drone nods forward/back |
| **Yaw** ($\psi$) | Vertical (Z) | $\psi$ | Rotating CW/CCW | Drone spins in place |

---

## 5. Motor Speed Control for Maneuvers

A quadcopter controls all motion purely by changing individual motor speeds. No mechanical servos or blade pitch changes are needed. Here's how:

### 5.1 Hovering

All 4 motors run at **equal speed**. Thrust equals weight. No net torque because paired CW and CCW motors cancel each other.

$$\sum \tau_{\text{CW}} = \sum \tau_{\text{CCW}}$$

### 5.2 Vertical Motion

| Command | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Increase all 4 motors equally | Climb (T > W) |
| Decrease all 4 motors equally | Descend (T < W) |

### 5.3 Yaw (Rotation)

Yaw is controlled by the **torque imbalance** between CW and CCW motors:

| Yaw Direction | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| **Yaw Right** (CW) | Increase CW motor speed / Decrease CCW speed |
| **Yaw Left** (CCW) | Increase CCW motor speed / Decrease CW speed |

### 5.4 Pitch (Forward/Backward)

| Motion | Motor Adjustment | Effect |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Forward** | Rear motors speed up | Nose tilts down, drone moves forward |
| **Backward** | Front motors speed up | Nose tilts up, drone moves backward |

Pitch tilts around the **lateral (Y) axis**.

### 5.5 Roll (Sideways)

| Motion | Motor Adjustment | Effect |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Roll Right** | Left-side motors speed up | Drone tilts right, moves right |
| **Roll Left** | Right-side motors speed up | Drone tilts left, moves left |

Roll tilts around the **longitudinal (X) axis**.

### Complete Maneuver Reference Table

| Maneuver | Motors Sped Up | Motors Slowed Down |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Climb | All 4 | — |
| Descend | — | All 4 |
| Forward | M3, M4 (rear) | M1, M2 (front) |
| Backward | M1, M2 (front) | M3, M4 (rear) |
| Roll Right | M2, M4 (left side) | M1, M3 (right side) |
| Roll Left | M1, M3 (right side) | M2, M4 (left side) |
| Yaw CW | CCW pair | CW pair |
| Yaw CCW | CW pair | CCW pair |

## 6. Thrust Equation

The aerodynamic thrust produced by a propeller:

$$T = C_T \cdot \rho \cdot n^2 \cdot D^4$$

where:
- $T$ = Thrust (N)
- $C_T$ = Thrust coefficient (depends on propeller geometry, typically 0.01–0.1)
- $\rho$ = Air density (kg/m³) — decreases at altitude, reducing thrust
- $n$ = Propeller rotational speed (rev/s)
- $D$ = Propeller diameter (m)

**Key observations:**
- Thrust scales with $n^2$ — doubling RPM quadruples thrust
- Thrust scales with $D^4$ — small increases in diameter have large effects
- Increasing pitch from 4.5″→5″ increases thrust by ~8.3%
- Increasing diameter by 1″ increases thrust by ~16%

## 7. Important Aerial Terminology Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **VTOL** | Vertical Take-Off and Landing — ability to take off without a runway |
| **Hover** | Sustained flight at constant altitude (T = W) |
| **Attitude** | The drone's orientation in 3D space (roll, pitch, yaw angles) |
| **Heading** | The direction the drone's nose is pointing |
| **Ground Speed** | Speed relative to the ground |
| **Air Speed** | Speed relative to the surrounding air |
| **Throttle** | Controls total thrust — the primary altitude control input |
| **Aileron** | Roll input (fixed-wing term, maps to left/right stick in drones) |
| **Elevator** | Pitch input (fixed-wing term) |
| **Rudder** | Yaw input (fixed-wing term) |
| **Waypoint** | A geographic coordinate in an autonomous flight plan |
| **Return to Home (RTH)** | Automated return to launch point on signal loss or low battery |
| **Arming** | Safety procedure to activate the drone's motors for flight |
| **Disarming** | Safety procedure to cut motor power after landing |

## 8. Flight Modes Overview

Modern flight controllers support multiple modes that change how the pilot's stick inputs are interpreted:

| Mode | Description | Use Case |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Stabilise (STAB)** | FC auto-levels roll/pitch — pilot must give constant input | Learning |
| **AltHold** | FC holds altitude; pilot controls roll/pitch/yaw | Photography |
| **Loiter / Position Hold** | GPS-locked position hold | Filming, inspection |
| **Auto** | FC executes pre-planned waypoint mission | Survey, mapping |
| **Acro (Rate Mode)** | No self-levelling — raw rate control | FPV racing |
| **RTL (Return to Launch)** | Automated return to home on trigger | Safety |
| **Land** | Automated controlled descent and landing | Convenience |
