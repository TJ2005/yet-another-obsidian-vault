## **High-Pitch Propellers**
High-pitch propellers have blades set at a **steeper angle of attack**, allowing them to move a larger volume of air per revolution. This increases thrust and speed capability at the cost of efficiency.

---

### **1. Increased Thrust**

* Higher pitch propellers generate more **thrust per revolution** than lower-pitch ones.
* Ideal for **high-speed** and **heavy-lift** applications.
* Common in **racing** or **industrial drones** needing strong acceleration.

$$ T \propto P \times n^2 \times D^4 $$
where $T$ = thrust, $P$ = pitch, $n$ = rotational speed, and $D$ = diameter.
A greater $P$ increases $T$ for the same $n$.

---

### **2. Aggressive Maneuvering**

* Allows for **rapid acceleration** and **quick altitude changes**.
* Preferred by **FPV racers** and **experienced pilots** seeking high responsiveness.

---

### **3. Shorter Flight Time**

* Higher pitch causes **increased drag** and **higher current draw**, reducing battery life.
* Energy efficiency decreases since the motor must exert more power to maintain RPM.

---

### **Summary**

| Feature     | Effect  | Best Use           |
| ----------- | ------- | ------------------ |
| Thrust      | High    | Heavy-lift / Speed |
| Power Draw  | High    | Racing             |
| Efficiency  | Low     | —                  |
| Flight Time | Shorter | —                  |
