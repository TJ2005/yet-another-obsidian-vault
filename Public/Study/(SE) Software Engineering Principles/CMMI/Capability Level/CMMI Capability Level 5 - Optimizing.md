## Characteristics
- Focuses on continually improving process performance
- Performance is improved in both ways - incremental and innovation
- Emphasizes studying the performance results across the organization to ensure that common causes or issues are identified and fixed

## Key Point
The process is continuously improved through both small incremental changes and innovative breakthroughs. The organization actively seeks ways to improve based on performance data and lessons learned.

## What This Means
The organization has a culture of continuous improvement. Performance data is analyzed across the entire organization to identify systemic issues and opportunities. Improvements come from both gradual refinements and innovative new approaches. The focus shifts from just controlling the process to actively optimizing it.
