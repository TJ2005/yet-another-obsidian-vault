## Definition
Incomplete process - partially or not performed.

## Characteristics
- One or more specific goals of process area are not met
- No generic goals are specified for this level
- Process is not performed or only partially performed

## Equivalence
This capability level is same as **Maturity Level 1** (Initial).

## Key Point
The process either doesn't exist or fails to meet its basic objectives. This is the baseline starting point before any process improvement begins.
