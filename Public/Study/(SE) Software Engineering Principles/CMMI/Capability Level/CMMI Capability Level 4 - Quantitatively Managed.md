## Characteristics
- Process is controlled using statistical and quantitative techniques
- Process performance and quality is understood in statistical terms and metrics
- Quantitative objectives for process quality and performance are established

## Key Point
The process moves beyond qualitative management to quantitative control. Statistical methods and data analysis drive decision-making and process control.

## What This Means
The organization uses numbers, statistics, and quantitative analysis to understand and control the process. Performance is measured objectively, and decisions are data-driven. Variability is understood and controlled through statistical methods.
