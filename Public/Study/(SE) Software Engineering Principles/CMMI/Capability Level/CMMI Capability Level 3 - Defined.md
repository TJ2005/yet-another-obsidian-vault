## Characteristics
- A defined process is managed and meets the organization's set of guidelines and standards
- Focus is process standardization

## Key Point
The process is not just managed for individual projects, but standardized across the organization. There are organizational guidelines and standards that all projects follow.

## What This Means
The organization has established "the way we do things here." Processes are documented with clear standards, procedures, and guidelines that are consistently applied across all projects and teams.
