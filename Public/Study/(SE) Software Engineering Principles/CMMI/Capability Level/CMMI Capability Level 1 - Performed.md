
## Characteristics
- Process performance may not be stable
- Objectives of quality, cost and schedule may not be met
- A capability level 1 process is expected to perform all specific and generic practices for this level
- Only a start-step for process improvement

## Key Point
The process is being performed and achieves its specific goals, but performance is unstable and objectives may not be consistently met. This is the foundation level where the process exists and functions, but without management controls.

## What This Means
Work gets done, but there's no guarantee of consistency, quality, or meeting targets. The process exists in practice but lacks the structure to ensure reliable results.
