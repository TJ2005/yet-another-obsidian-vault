## Characteristics
- Process is planned, monitored and controlled
- Managing the process by ensuring that objectives are achieved
- Objectives include model and other factors: cost, quality, schedule
- Actively managing processing with the help of metrics

## Key Point
The process is now under management control. It's planned before execution, monitored during execution, and controlled to ensure objectives are met. Metrics are used to track performance.

## What This Means
The organization takes control of the process by setting objectives, tracking progress, and making adjustments as needed. Performance becomes more predictable through active management and measurement.
