# CMMI Maturity Levels Overview

## Staged Representation
In CMMI with staged representation, there are five maturity levels that represent an evolutionary path for process improvement.

## The Five Levels

### Level 1: Initial
- Processes poorly managed or controlled
- Unpredictable outcomes, ad hoc and chaotic
- No KPAs defined
- Lowest quality and highest risk

### Level 2: Managed
- Requirements are managed
- Processes are planned and controlled
- Projects managed according to documented plans
- Risk lower than Initial, quality better than Initial

### Level 3: Defined
- Processes well characterized with standards and procedures
- Medium quality and medium risk
- Focus on process standardization

### Level 4: Quantitatively Managed
- Quantitative objectives for performance and quality set
- Process performance analyzed quantitatively
- Higher quality, lower risk

### Level 5: Optimizing
- Continuous improvement (incremental and innovative)
- Highest quality of processes
- Lowest risk in processes and performance

## Progression Pattern
- **Quality**: Increases from Level 1 → Level 5
- **Risk**: Decreases from Level 1 → Level 5
- **Control**: Increases from ad hoc → optimized
- **Predictability**: Increases from chaotic → continuous improvement
