## Characteristics
- Processes are unpredictable and reactive
- Work gets completed but is often delayed and over budget
- Success depends on individual heroics
- Ad-hoc approach to work
- No Key Process Areas Defined ( **KPA** )

## Risk & Quality
- **Risk**: Highest
- **Quality**: Unpredictable and inconsistent

## Key Point
Organizations at this level lack stable processes and rely heavily on individual effort rather than organizational capability.
