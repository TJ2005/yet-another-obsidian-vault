## Aim

To study DPDP Act 2023.

## Learning Outcomes

- Explain the need for privacy Act
- Describe rights and duties of data principal
- Describe compliance framework

## Theory

The purpose of the DPDP Act is to "provide for the processing of digital personal data in a manner that recognizes both the right of individuals to protect their personal data and the need to process personal data for lawful purposes".

**Key Definitions:**
- **Data** — Representation of information, facts, concepts, opinions or instructions in a manner suitable for communication, interpretation or processing by humans or by automated means
- **Data Fiduciary** — Person who determines the purpose and means of processing of personal data
- **Data Principal** — Individual to whom the personal data relates
- **Data Processor** — Any person who processes personal data on behalf of a data fiduciary
