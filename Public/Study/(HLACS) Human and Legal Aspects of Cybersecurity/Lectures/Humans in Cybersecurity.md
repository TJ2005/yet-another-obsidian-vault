# Humans in Cybersecurity

> [!QUOTE] Bruce Schneier, *Secrets & Lies*
> "If you think technology can solve your security problems, then you don't understand the problems and you don't understand the technology."

## Humans are the last line of defense

- **Determine the need** for new technologies and processes
- **Select and purchase** the technologies to implement
- **Define process standards** to be followed
- **Review and tweak** settings of business and security technologies
- **Design and code** in-house applications
- **Review agreements** with third-party organisations
- **Decide responses** to suspicious incidents and tailgating attempts
- Make **conscious and unconscious decisions** daily about systems and information
- Humans = **employees, contractors, shareholders, customers**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Security is everyone's responsibility.

## Security–Usability–Functionality triangle

- Three competing corners — strengthening one weakens the others
- **Hospital scenario**: password + fingerprint + OTP + smart card before every patient record → secure, but *"would patients survive?"*
- **Usability is itself a security requirement**
- Protection scales with criticality:

| System | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Nuclear plant / Military network | Maximum security |
| Banking app | High security + usable |
| WhatsApp / Google Docs / Instagram | Usability-weighted |

## Human issues in cybersecurity

- **Lack of knowledge and awareness**
- **A relaxed culture**
- **Ineffective training programs**
- **Lack of management training**
- Environment that does not **encourage teamwork**
- **Cultural differences**

## Types of cybersecurity behaviours

- Classified on two axes — **intention** (unintentional ↔ intentional) × **outcome** (negative ↔ positive)

| | Negative outcome | Positive outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unintentional** | Negligence / naive mistakes | Basic hygiene |
| **Intentional** | Malicious | Aware / assurance |
