# The 7 Dimensions of Security Culture

> [!INFO] Framework
> Security culture decomposes into seven measurable dimensions — the basis for [[Measuring a CSC Program]] and diagnosis via [[Culture Failure Scenarios]].

|  #  | Dimension            | Definition                                                                                    |
| :-: | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Attitude**         | Feelings and beliefs employees hold toward security protocols and issues                      |
|  2  | **Behaviours**       | Actions/activities of employees with direct or indirect impact on organisational security     |
|  3  | **Cognition**        | Understanding, knowledge and awareness of security issues and activities                      |
|  4  | **Communication**    | Quality of channels to discuss security topics, promote belonging, support incident reporting |
|  5  | **Compliance**       | Knowledge of written security policies **and the extent employees follow them**               |
|  6  | **Responsibilities** | How employees perceive their role — as critical to sustaining or endangering security         |
|  7  | **Norms**            | Knowledge of and adherence to **unwritten rules** of conduct in the organisation              |

- Memory grouping:
	- **Internal to person** → attitude, cognition
	- **Visible action** → behaviours, compliance, norms
	- **Organisational fabric** → communication, responsibilities
