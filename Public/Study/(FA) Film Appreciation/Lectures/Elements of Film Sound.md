# Elements of Film Sound

## Diegetic vs non-diegetic

| Type | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diegetic** | Sound that exists inside the film's world — characters can hear it | Dialogue, footsteps, a radio in the scene |
| **Non-diegetic** | Sound outside the film's world — only the audience hears it | Score, narration, laugh tracks |

## Soundtrack vs score

- **Soundtrack**: the assembled music of a film (can include existing songs)
- **Score**: composed music for the film
- Both are used to **build emotion** and **complement the scene** — and can acquire central importance (see the Godfather theme in [[Film and Other Art Forms]])

## Narration and perspective

- Framing and camera movement are **tied to the character's emotions**
- Sound helps translate **internal thoughts into external sources**
- **Internality through expressions** — inner state shown, not told
