# Mise-en-Scène

> [!INFO] Definition
> Everything placed in front of the camera — the arrangement of actors, scenery and props. From theatre (early 19th century); popularised in cinema by French critic/theorist **André Bazin** — "an important aesthetic revolution that choreographed movement *within the scene* rather than through editing."

- Bazin's mise-en-scène vs montage: **move the camera / choreograph in front of it** instead of cutting between shots

## Two modes

| Mode | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Naturalistic** | The film world matches our own — familiar, realistic |
| **Theatrical** | Exaggerated worlds, not meant to be normal — exaggerated sets, costumes… |

## The five questions

1. How are the actors positioned?
2. How is the scene lit?
3. What are the shooting angles?
4. What are we seeing that the characters may not be?
5. Is there movement — in the scene or of the camera?

> [!note] Composition is everything
> Each frame should be telling the audience something — literally or inferred.

## Components

| Component | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Production design** | Set production, colour |
| **Cinematography** | Camera movement, lighting techniques, composition, transitions, editing |
| **Blocking** | Actor's movement and engagement with the environment |

### The mise-en-scène elements

Location (themes) • Props • Staging • Colour • Sound & music • Camera angle • **Lighting: three-point, low-key, high-key** • Actor blocking • Framing • Composition

![[p30_img_16.png]]

| Lighting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Three-point** (key + fill + backlight) | Standard, flattering, legible |
| **Low-key** | Strong shadows, high contrast — noir, dread, secrecy |
| **High-key** | Even, bright, shadowless — comedies, optimism |

## Analysis checklist — read a scene by

| Lens | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| **Setting** | How is physical space used to reflect the inner world of characters — class, culture, routine? |
| **Lighting & colour** | Purpose? Warm vs cool colours? |
| **Costume & props** | What do clothing choices indicate? What props? |
| **Actor movement & framing** | What do movement and expressions say? How are they framed? |
| **Theme — atmosphere** | How is the director's vision communicated through atmosphere? |
