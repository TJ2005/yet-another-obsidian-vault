# Film and Other Art Forms

> [!INFO] The claim
> Film borrows from the other arts but is its own composite medium — adaptation, not adoption. Borrowing carries a specific language: the **semiotics of filmmaking**.

## Context: what counts as "art"

- The ancients recognised **seven activities** as arts: History, Poetry, Comedy, Tragedy, Music, Dance, Astronomy
- Poetry split into **Lyric / Dramatic / Epic** → modern literary arts
- The curriculum later split into **art and science**; new disciplines (architecture, sculpting…) appeared
- Photography developed closer to the **sciences** than to art — it is **mimesis** (representation)
- Cinema: a medium of **communication** that mediates our experience (see [[What is Cinema]])

## Film × each art form

| Art | Relationship to film | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Literature** | Adaptations are common: word → film (narrative, setting, description). Tension between the **image and the story's elements**; writing styles change too | [The Perks of Being a Wallflower](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OMSkavUrzHM) |
| **Theatre** | Similar: storytelling, runtime, performance. Theatre = **voice**; film = **expressions**. Evolution went scene → act → back to scene (realism) | [Hamlet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6CLdCl9TB0) vs [Haider (2014)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RJAJdYw3ctw) |
| **Architecture** | Setting (buildings/design) carries the message; films explore architectural themes or use iconic buildings as significant elements | [Blade Runner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ePbK0tiVWKY), Hogwarts |
| **Music** | Enhances the scene, provokes emotion; gives rhythm, continuity, flow. Iconic soundtracks enter popular culture | [Godfather theme](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Pf8BkFLBRw), [character-development cues](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQl7zYkEP6M) |
| **Dance** | Musicals integrate dance + music + acting to tell the story; dance adds expression and artistry | [La La Land](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xVVqlm8Fq3Y), [Dholida](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uv9Dv6fzg9w) |

## Semiotics of film

- **Signifier and signified** — the sign represents a referent
- **Icon**: resembles its referent (smoke → fire)
- **Index**: points at / indicates something else — causal or direct link (**show, don't tell**)
- **Symbol**: arbitrary cultural convention

> [!question] Analyse
> What icons does Haider use? What icons does The Perks of Being a Wallflower use?

## Sound and perspective

- **Diegetic** vs **non-diegetic** sound; soundtrack vs score — built to create emotion and complement the scene, can acquire central importance
- **Narration/perspective**: framing and camera movement tied to a character's emotions — translating **internal thoughts to external sources**; internality through expressions

## In conclusion

- Adaptation — **not adoption**
- Film is non-linear / non-sequential where needed
- Shape and form matter — "shape/form?"
- Borrowing from other art forms = the specific language of **semiotics of filmmaking**
