# Structuring Drivers

### **Logistical Drivers**

The physical "hardware" of the supply chain.

- **Facilities:** Locations where products are stored or made. More facilities increase **responsiveness** (closeness to customer) but decrease **efficiency** (higher costs).
    
- **Inventory:** Raw materials and finished goods. High inventory ensures **availability** but ties up **capital**.
    
- **Transportation:** Moving goods between facilities. Faster modes (air) improve **speed**; slower modes (sea/rail) improve **cost-efficiency**.
    

---

### **Cross-Functional Drivers**

The "software" that manages the physical assets.

- **Information:** Data used for forecasting and coordination. It is the most important driver for reducing **uncertainty**.
    
- **Sourcing:** Deciding who performs tasks (in-house vs. outsourcing). Affects both **quality** and **overhead**.
    
- **Pricing:** Setting costs to influence customer demand. Helps **level out** demand spikes to optimize capacity.
