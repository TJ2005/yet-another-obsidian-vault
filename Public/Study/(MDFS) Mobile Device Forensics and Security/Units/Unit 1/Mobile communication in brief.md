## 1.1.1 Old vs Modern Networks (Quick Absorption)

### 1.1.1.1 Old (Mainframe / Early Networks)

* Batch-based processing
* No real-time interaction
* Data submitted → results received later (often next day)
* Static, centralized systems
* No mobility support

### 1.1.1.2 Modern Mobile Networks

* Real-time, continuous communication
* Support mobility and dynamic environments
* Examples:

  * Highway navigation (GIS updates while moving)
  * Patient data transmission in ambulances
  * Aircraft/spacecraft telemetry
  * Mobile video conferencing and news delivery
* Core shift:
  **from delayed computation → real-time, mobile, device-to-device communication**

## 1.1.3 Scope of Mobile Networks

### 1.1.3.1 Growth Drivers

* Internet and laptop usage increasing
* Wireless LAN growth (Wi-Fi, next-gen systems)
* High demand for low-rate data
* Military and security requirements
* Interdisciplinary applications emerging
