# 1.9.3 5G — Fifth Generation Mobile Networks

> [!abstract] What This Note Covers
> - 5G goals: massive speed, low latency, huge device density
> - Core technologies: NR, mmWave, massive MIMO
> - Applications: IoT, AR/VR, eMBB, mission-critical

> [!seealso] Related Notes
> - [[4G Network#1.9.2 4G Network]]
> - [[3G Network#1.9.1 3G — Third Generation Mobile Networks]]
> - [[Wireless System Distributed Control and Spectrum#1.4.6 Standards]]

> [!info] Quick Facts
> - Typical rollout timeframe: ~2019 onward.

## 1.9.3.1 Introduction

* Rollout: **~2019 onward**

---

## 1.9.3.2 Key Features

* Ultra-fast speeds: **1 Gbps → 100 Gbps**
* Ultra-low latency: **~1 ms**
* Massive device connectivity (IoT scale)

---

## 1.9.3.3 Core Technologies

### 1.9.3.3.1 New Radio (NR)

* 5G air interface
* Flexible across frequencies

---

### 1.9.3.3.2 Millimeter Wave (mmWave)

* Frequency ≥ 24 GHz
* Very high bandwidth

**Pros**

* High data rates
* Large capacity

**Cons**

* Short range
* Easily blocked

---

### 1.9.3.3.3 Massive MIMO

> [!tip] Linkback
> - Related concept: [[Wireless Networks MultiPlexing in Dimensions#1.5.2.3 Space]]

* Many antennas at base station
* Multiple users served simultaneously

**Benefits**

* Higher throughput
* Better coverage
* Improved spectral efficiency

---

## 1.9.3.4 Applications

### 1.9.3.4.1 IoT and Smart Cities

* Smart sensors
* Connected infrastructure

---

### 1.9.3.4.2 AR / VR

* Real-time immersive applications

---

### 1.9.3.4.3 Enhanced Mobile Broadband (eMBB)

* Ultra HD streaming
* Fast downloads

---

### 1.9.3.4.4 Mission-Critical Applications

* Remote surgery
* Industrial automation
* Emergency services

---

## 1.9.3.5 Impact

* Enables automation and smart systems
* Supports massive connectivity
* Drives next-gen applications

---

## 1.9.3.6 Core Difference (One Line)

* **4G:** High-speed mobile internet
* **5G:** Ultra-fast, ultra-low latency, massive connectivity platform
