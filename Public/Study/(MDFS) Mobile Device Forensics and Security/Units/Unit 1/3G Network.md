## 1.9.1.1 Introduction

* Launched: **2001 (Japan, NTT DoCoMo)**
* Shift from:

  * **2G → voice-centric**
  * **3G → data-centric**

---

## 1.9.1.2 Key Features

### 1.9.1.2.1 Data Transmission

* Higher data transfer rates than 2G
* Supports **large volume data transfer**
* Enables mobile internet usage

---

### 1.9.1.2.2 Speed

* Broadband-like experience on mobile
* Faster than 2G (varies by implementation)

---

### 1.9.1.2.3 Capabilities

* Internet browsing
* Email services
* Multimedia applications

---

## 1.9.1.3 Core Technologies

### 1.9.1.3.1 CDMA2000 (Code Division Multiple Access 2000)

* Based on **[[Wireless Networks MultiPlexing in Dimensions#1.5.2.4 Code|CDMA technique]]**
* Developed by **Qualcomm**
* Widely used in:

  * North America
  * Parts of Asia

#### 1.9.1.3.1.1 Features

* Higher data speeds (vs 2G)
* Improved capacity
* Better voice quality

---

### 1.9.1.3.2 WCDMA (Wideband CDMA)

* Uses **wider bandwidth** than CDMA2000
* Part of **UMTS (Universal Mobile Telecommunications System)**
* Adopted by GSM networks globally

#### 1.9.1.3.2.1 Features

* Higher data transfer rates
* Better multimedia support

---

## 1.9.1.4 Data Rates

* Range:

  * **Hundreds of Kbps → Several Mbps**
* Depends on:

  * Network implementation
  * Technology (CDMA2000 / WCDMA)

---

## 1.9.1.5 Capabilities

### 1.9.1.5.1 Enhanced Mobile Internet

* Practical web browsing
* Email access
* Online applications

---

### 1.9.1.5.2 Video Calling and Streaming

* Real-time video communication
* Multimedia content delivery

---

### 1.9.1.5.3 Advanced Applications

* Location-based services
* Mobile gaming
* Interactive multimedia

---

## 1.9.1.6 Core Understanding

* 3G = **transition from voice → data**
* Enabled:

  * Mobile internet
  * Multimedia communication
* Foundation for:

  * Modern smartphone ecosystem
