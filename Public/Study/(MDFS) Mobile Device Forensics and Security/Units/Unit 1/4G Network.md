## 1.9.2.1 4G — Fourth Generation Mobile Networks

### 1.9.2.1.1 Introduction

* Launch: **2009 (Sweden)**
* Standard: **ITU IMT-Advanced**
* Technologies:

  * LTE (dominant)
  * WiMAX (limited adoption)

---

### 1.9.2.1.2 Key Features

* High data speeds: **100 Mbps → 1 Gbps**
* Low latency (improved over 3G)
* High spectral efficiency

---

### 1.9.2.1.3 LTE (Long-Term Evolution)

* Primary 4G standard
* Evolution of 3G (GSM/UMTS compatible)

#### 1.9.2.1.3.1 Features

* High-speed data transmission
* Low latency → better responsiveness
* Efficient spectrum usage → higher capacity

---

### 1.9.2.1.4 Capabilities

#### 1.9.2.1.4.1 High-Speed Internet

* Fast browsing
* Large downloads

#### 1.9.2.1.4.2 HD Video Streaming

* Smooth playback
* Minimal buffering

#### 1.9.2.1.4.3 Mobile Gaming and AR

* Lower lag
* Real-time interaction

---

### 1.9.2.1.5 Impact

* Enabled mobile-first ecosystem
* Growth of streaming and apps
* Improved user experience
