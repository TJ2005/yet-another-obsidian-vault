# Drone Technology Index

## Course Overview
This course covers the fundamentals of drone technology, from basic UAV concepts to autonomous control systems. Total Duration: **30 hours**

---

## Unit 1: Introduction to UAV Systems (6 Hours)

### Syllabus
- Introduction to Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV)
- Aviation history and overview of UAV systems
- UAV fundamentals, definitions and terminology
- Classes and missions of UAVs
- Examples of UAV systems (very small, small, medium, large)
- Parts of the drone (Hardware, Software, Mechanical subsystems)
- Ground based controllers
- Use cases in Military, Industrial, Commercial applications

### Lecture Notes
- [[Drone]] - Definition, classification, and key components
- [[Aerial Terminology in Drones]] - Flight physics and motion control
- [[Drone tech notes for m2]] - Transmitter, receiver, and flight controller basics

---

## Unit 2: Drone System Design Flow (8 Hours)

### Syllabus
- System Design: requirement specification, architecture
- Mechanical design: Propeller, X-frame, Mylar, MB, Shielding, TIM, Heat spreader, FPC
- Hardware Design: Drone hardware block diagram, system stack-up
- Drone Power Architecture and Estimation
- SOC power requirements, power devices
- Linear regulator, Switching regulator, battery estimation
- Battery constraints: watt hours, energy density, capacity, and cost

### Lecture Notes
- [[Propellors & Motors]] - Propeller and motor fundamentals
- [[High Pitch Propellors]] - High-pitch propeller characteristics
- [[Low Pitch Propellors]] - Low-pitch propeller characteristics
- [[High Pitch vs Low Pitch Propellors]] - Comparative analysis
- [[High KV vs Low KV Motors]] - Motor KV ratings explained
- [[Thrust Dependencies]] - Thrust relationship with KV, propellers
- [[Electronic Speed Controller]] - ESC fundamentals and working
- [[Key Factors in choosing ESC]] - ESC selection criteria
- [[Additional Factors for choosing ESC]] - BEC, budget, compatibility
- [[Power Distribution in a drone]] - Power distribution board and system
- [[Drone Batteries]] - Battery types, performance, and safety

---

## Unit 3: Drone Hardware Devices (6 Hours)

### Syllabus
- UAV sensors: accelerometers, rate gyros, pressure sensors, digital compasses, GPS
- Communication Module: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth
- Fundamentals of 3G/4G mobile communication
- RF communications
- Drone communication system
- Drone imaging system
- Flight Controllers based on MCU/MPU, FPGA

### Lecture Notes
- [[Drone Technlogy Sensors]] - ADXL335 accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer
- [[Drone tech notes for m2]] - Flight controller functions and communication systems

---

## Unit 4: Autonomous Control of Mini Quadrotor Vehicle (10 Hours)

### Syllabus
- Introduction of Mini Quadrotor Vehicle
- Description of the Experimental Platform
- Embedded Control System
- Sensor units, Control unit
- Wireless Camera and Modem unit
- Design of controller and its modelling
- Drone security threats: terrorist attacks, illegal surveillance, reconnaissance
- Smuggling, electronic snooping, mid-air collisions

- Autonomous control systems
- Controller design and modelling
- Security considerations and threat analysis
