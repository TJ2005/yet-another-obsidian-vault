## Course Objective

Offer the tools and techniques of film appreciation and expose students to different genres of world cinema to make them avid, critical and active viewers. Content, form and context combine to create meaning in film (CO1); informed personal response (CO2); technical production, grammar, cultural and historical context (CO3).

## Assignments

### Assignment 1 — Video Review (15 marks, week 10)

Record a review of a film clip analysing **cinematography, sound, mise-en-scène, dialogues, themes and symbolism**. No marks if the review is only a summary.

- Cinematography and Sound: 3 | Mise-en-scène: 3 | Themes and Symbolism: 4 (per criteria up to excellent 5 marks)

### Assignment 2 — No-Faces Short Film (10 marks)

Character-driven 90-120 second film where **no faces are shown** (hands, feet, objects, silhouettes). Convey status/power/meaning through visual cues, sound design, performance and editing. Rough cut: week 5; final cut: week 12. Submit MP4 + teamwork contribution + statement of intent.

- Statement of Intent: 2 | Mise-en-Scène: 4 | Cinematography: 4 | Sound: 4 | Timely submission: 1 (per criterion up to 4-5 marks)
