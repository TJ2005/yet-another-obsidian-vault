# Human Centered Cybersecurity - Course Policy

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Code | 702IT0E034 |
| Teaching scheme | 2 lectures + 2 practical hours/week |
| Credits | 3 |
| Evaluation | ICA 50; TEE 100 scaled to 50 |
| Prerequisite | Fundamental knowledge of cybersecurity |
| Laboratory work | 8 to 10 exercises based on the syllabus |

## Course Objective

Understand human interaction with security technology and apply economics, game theory, personality, marketing and behavioural science to cybersecurity decisions and awareness.

## Units

| Unit | Title | Duration | Contents |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Economics | 6 hours | Classical and behavioural economics, asymmetric information, auctions, security economics, Kahneman-Tversky, heuristics and biases. |
| 2 | Game Theory and Cybersecurity | 8 hours | Payoffs, matrix games, mixed strategies, saddle points, dominated strategies and applications. |
| 3 | Personality Theory and Cybersecurity | 5 hours | Personality traits, cybersecurity profiling, profiling matrices and ABCD analysis. |
| 4 | Marketing and Communications | 6 hours | Communication, marketing parallels, awareness campaigns, tracking and effectiveness. |
| 5 | Behaviour Management | 5 hours | System 1/2, human nature, Fogg model, nudges, frames, behaviour design/debugging and measurement. |
|  | **Total** | **30 hours** |  |
