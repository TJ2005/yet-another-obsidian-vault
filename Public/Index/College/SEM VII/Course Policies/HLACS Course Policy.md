# Human and Legal Aspects of Cybersecurity - Course Policy

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Code | 702IT0C027 |
| Teaching scheme | 2 lectures + 2 practical hours/week |
| Credits | 3 |
| Evaluation | ICA 50; TEE 100 scaled to 50 |
| Prerequisite | Fundamental knowledge of cybersecurity |
| Laboratory work | 8 to 10 exercises based on the syllabus |

## Course Objective

Understand how user behaviour affects organisational security, how to build security education, training and awareness programs, and how national and international cyber laws guide security programs and solutions.

## Units

| Unit | Title | Duration | Contents |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | 6 hours | Human role in securing assets; security-usability triangle; cybersecurity behaviours; SETA; System 1 and 2 thinking; security fatigue; heuristics and biases; ADDIE. |
| 2 | Cybersecurity Culture | 8 hours | Need for CSC; program development and implementation; organisational requirements; elements, resources and measurement. |
| 3 | Cyber Laws | 4 hours | Indian judicial system; types and need for laws; cybercrimes, criminals, classification and incident reporting. |
| 4 | Indian IT Act 2000 and ITAA 2008 | 4 hours | Features, scope, sections, offences, penalties, appeal, amendments, appellate tribunal and cases. |
| 5 | Global Cyber and Privacy Regulations | 8 hours | SOX, GLBA, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR and Indian privacy bill. |
|  | **Total** | **30 hours** |  |
