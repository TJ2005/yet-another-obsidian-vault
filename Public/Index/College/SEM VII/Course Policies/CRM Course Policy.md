# Cybersecurity Risk Management - Course Policy

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Code | 702IT0C028 |
| Teaching scheme | 2 lectures + 2 practical hours/week |
| Credits | 3 |
| Evaluation | ICA 50; TEE 100 scaled to 50 |
| Prerequisite | Fundamental knowledge of cybersecurity |
| Laboratory work | 8 to 10 exercises based on the syllabus |

## Course Objective

Understand qualitative and quantitative cybersecurity risk assessment, articulate cyber risk as business consequences, and apply risk-management, continuity and recovery frameworks.

## Units

| Unit | Title                                |     Duration | Contents                                                                                                            | Status |
| ---- | ------------------------------------ | -----------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| 1    | Risk Management Fundamentals         |      3 hours | Threats, vulnerabilities, impact, lifecycle, identification and risk-management techniques.                         |        |
| 2    | Risk Assessment                      |      4 hours | Components and types of assessment; identification and evaluation of threats, vulnerabilities and countermeasures.  |        |
| 3    | Risk Management Planning             |      6 hours | Objectives, scope, responsibilities, reporting, action plans, compliance, prioritisation and cost-benefit analysis. |        |
| 4    | Business Impact Analysis             |      4 hours | Introduction, objectives, scope and BIA process.                                                                    |        |
| 5    | BCP and DRP                          |      4 hours | Need, elements and best practices for business continuity and disaster recovery plans.                              |        |
| 6    | Risk Management Frameworks           |      6 hours | NIST RMF, CERT OCTAVE, ISO 27000 series and FAIR.                                                                   |        |
| 7    | Risk, Resilience and Decision-Making |      3 hours | Cyber risk versus resilience; risk-informed and resilience-informed decisions.                                      |        |
|      | **Total**                            | **30 hours** |                                                                                                                     |        |
