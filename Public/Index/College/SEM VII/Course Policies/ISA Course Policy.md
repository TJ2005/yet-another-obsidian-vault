# Information Systems Audit - Course Policy

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Code | 702IT0E033 |
| Teaching scheme | 2 lectures + 2 practical hours/week |
| Credits | 3 |
| Evaluation | ICA 50; TEE 100 scaled to 50 |
| Prerequisite | Fundamentals of cybersecurity and risk management |
| Alignment | ISACA CISA certification |
| Laboratory work | 8 to 10 exercises based on the syllabus |

## Course Objective

Develop the skills required to govern and control enterprise IT and perform effective information-systems security audits.

## Units

| Unit | Title | Duration | Contents |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Auditing Information Systems | 5 hours | Audit function, ISACA standards/guidelines, risk analysis, controls, self-assessment and evolving audit process. |
| 2 | Governance and Management of IT | 7 hours | Governance, monitoring, strategy, maturity, investment, policies, risk, organisation and business continuity. |
| 3 | IS Acquisition, Development and Implementation | 7 hours | Project management, development, acquisition, maintenance, improvement, application controls and audits. |
| 4 | IS Operations, Maintenance and Support | 5 hours | Operations, hardware, architecture, software, networks, infrastructure audits and disaster recovery. |
| 5 | Protection of Information Assets | 4 hours | Security management, logical/network/physical access, environmental controls and mobile computing. |
| 6 | Security Standards Case Study | 2 hours | Case study based on standards such as PCI DSS and ISO 27001. |
|  | **Total** | **30 hours** |  |
