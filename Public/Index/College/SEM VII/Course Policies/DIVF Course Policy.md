# Digital Image and Video Forensics - Course Policy

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Code | 702IT0E032 |
| Teaching scheme | 2 lectures + 2 practical hours/week |
| Credits | 3 |
| Evaluation | ICA 50; TEE 100 scaled to 50 |
| Prerequisite | Digital Forensics Fundamentals |
| Laboratory work | 8 to 10 exercises based on the syllabus |

## Course Objective

Identify, collect, handle, analyse and authenticate image and video evidence while preserving integrity and following forensic best practices.

## Units

| Unit | Title | Duration | Contents |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | 4 hours | Legal evidence, forensic principles, protocols and guidelines for collection, recovery, enhancement, analysis and interpretation. |
| 2 | Image and Video Processing and Analytics | 8 hours | Formats, standards, recording losses and artefacts, quality metrics, video categories and analytics algorithms. |
| 3 | Authentication and Tamper Detection | 12 hours | Source/content authentication; hashes, hex, metadata and ELA; splicing, removal, resampling, frame tampering, recompression and transcoding. |
| 4 | Deepfake Detection | 6 hours | Technology, creation process and detection methods. |
|  | **Total** | **30 hours** |  |
