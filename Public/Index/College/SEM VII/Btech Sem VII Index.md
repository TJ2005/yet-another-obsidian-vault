## Course Policy

All theory subjects carry **3 credits** with **2 lecture + 2 practical hours per week** and a 30-hour syllabus. The Capstone Project carries **4 credits** and **8 practical hours per week**.

### Human and Legal Aspects of Cybersecurity

Full policy: [[HLACS Course Policy]]

| Unit | Topic | Duration | Information |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | 6 hours | Human role in security, behaviour, SETA, decision-making, fatigue, biases and ADDIE. |
| 2 | Cybersecurity Culture | 8 hours | CSC program requirements, implementation, resources and measurement. |
| 3 | Cyber Laws | 4 hours | Indian judicial system, types of law, cybercrime classification and incident reporting. |
| 4 | Indian IT Act 2000 and ITAA 2008 | 4 hours | Scope, sections, offences, penalties, amendments, tribunal and cases. |
| 5 | Global Cyber and Privacy Regulations | 8 hours | SOX, GLBA, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR and the Indian privacy bill. |
