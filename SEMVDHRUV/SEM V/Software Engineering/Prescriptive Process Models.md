## Key Differences

### Maturity Levels vs Capability Levels
- **Maturity Levels**: Apply to the entire organization (staged approach)
- **Capability Levels**: Apply to individual process areas (continuous approach)

### Application
- **Staged (Maturity)**: Organization-wide improvement path
- **Continuous (Capability)**: Process-specific improvement, allows different processes to be at different levels

## Connection
Both representations aim for process improvement but take different approaches:
- Staged = Organizational maturity
- Continuous = Process capability
