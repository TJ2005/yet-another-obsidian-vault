
## Requirement Engineering Processes

Requirement Engineering is a structured process for defining, analyzing, designing and managing software requirements to ensure successful system development. This process uses various methods, and principles to fill in the communication gap between the technical team and non-technical stakeholders.

Requirement Engineering involves several steps that needs to be followed:
1. Inception: The purpose of this step is to establish a shared understanding between the stakeholders and developers about
	1. What the software is supposed to do
	2. Who will use it
	3. Why is it needed
	4. What constraints and risks exist
2. Elicitation: This revolves around gathering initial information about the software that needs to be developed and understanding the problem space
	1. Understand the business context
	2. Identify stake holders and their expectations
	3. high-level functional and non-functional requirements
	4. Identify constraints
3. Elaboration
4. Negotiation
5. Specification
6. Validation 
7. Management
