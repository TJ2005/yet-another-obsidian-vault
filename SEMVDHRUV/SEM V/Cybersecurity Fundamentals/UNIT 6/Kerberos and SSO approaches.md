Kerberos is a trusted third party authentication protocol that can be used to provide a single sign on solution and to provide protection to logon credentials.

It is often used by organizations that have too many services that require authentication. 

There are majorly two versions 4,5

Its first report identified requirements as: 
- Secure 
- Reliable 
- Transparent
- Scalable


## Components
Kerberos Authentication mechanism revolves around a trusted server/servers.
- KDC
- Ticket Granting Server
- Authentication Service
