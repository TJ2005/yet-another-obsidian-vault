# Threats

- **Definition:** An object / Person / Something that poses a constant danger to an asset.
- By knowing ones own self's threat one can be ready when things go south.
- **Examples of Threats**
	- Human Failure
	- Human Error
	- Intellectual Property Compromising
	- Deliberate acts of
		- Espionage 
		- Tresspassing
		- Extortion
		- Sabotage
		- Vandalism
		- Thefts
	- Force of Nature


## Classification of Attack

- **Passive Attack** : Eaves Dropping or monitoring of transmissions
	- Obtain message contents
	- Monitor active traffic flows
- **Active Attacks:** Modification of data stream
	- Masquerade
	- Replay previous messages
	- Denial of service


- **Examples**
	- release message content
	- Traffic Analysis
	- Replay
	- Social Engineering
	- Phishing
	- Spoofing
		- Web Spoofing
		- Email Spoofing
	- Malware


## Advanced Persistent Threat

- **Reconaissance:** Attacker leverages multiple factors to identify the target 
- **Incursion:** Attackers tries to get in by social engineering
- **Discovery:** Trying to map out the internal defence to curate an attack kill chain
- **Capture:** Capturing of information and installation of the malware
- **Exfiltration:** Captured Information is then sent back or the malware gives control of the device to the attacker
