
# **PDDMDR – Six Ways to Defend Against Attacks**

## **1. Prevent Attack**

Stop the attack *before it ever starts*. This focuses on eliminating vulnerabilities.

**Examples:**

* **Code Review:** Developers manually inspect code to remove bugs or insecure logic that attackers could exploit.
* **Penetration Testing:** Ethical hackers simulate attacks to uncover weak points before real attackers find them.
* **Strong Authentication:** Using MFA so attackers cannot log in even if they steal a password.
* **Regular Patching:** Updating systems to close known security holes.

---

## **2. Deter Attack**

Make attackers *think twice* by increasing the risk or difficulty of attempting the attack.

**Examples:**

* **Warning banners** (“Unauthorized access will be prosecuted”) on login screens.
* **Visible security controls** like CCTV cameras or monitored firewalls.
* **Rate-limiting login attempts** so brute-force attacks become slow and frustrating.
* **Legal consequences** outlined in company policy.

---

## **3. Deflect Attack**

Redirect attackers toward a fake or less valuable target. This wastes their time and protects real assets.

**Examples:**

* **Honeypots:** Fake servers that look real and attract attackers so you can monitor their behavior.
* **Honeytokens:** Fake credentials or files that trigger alerts when used.
* **Sandbox environments:** Attackers are deceived into interacting with isolated fake environments.

---

## **4. Mitigate Attack**

Reduce the damage when an attack *does* occur. The goal is to limit the impact.

**Examples:**

* **Incident Response Plan:** A predefined step-by-step plan for containment and control.
* **Network Segmentation:** Even if one part is compromised, the attacker cannot move easily to others.
* **Backups:** Minimizing data loss if ransomware encrypts files.
* **Rate limiting:** Minimizes impact of DDoS attacks.

---

## **5. Detect Attack**

Identify attacks in progress or after they occur.

**Examples:**

* **IDS/IPS (Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems):** Tools that alert you when suspicious activity happens.
* **Log Monitoring:** Reviewing system logs to detect unauthorized access attempts.
* **SIEM systems:** Automatically analyze security events in real time.
* **File Integrity Monitoring:** Alerts you if critical files are changed.

---

## **6. Recover from Attack**

Restore systems and services back to normal after an attack.

**Examples:**

* **Restoring from backups** after ransomware or data corruption.
* **Rebuilding compromised systems** to a clean state.
* **Post-incident analysis** to ensure the attacker is removed and weaknesses are fixed.
* **Disaster recovery procedures** to resume operations quickly.
