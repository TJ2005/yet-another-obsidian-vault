# Controls

Controls are the means and ways to block a threat, which tries to exploit one or more vulnerabilities. They work by blocking or reducing the ability of an attacker to exploit vulnerabilities, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of assets.

# Attacks

Attacks are deliberate actions by adversaries that attempt to exploit vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or people. Their goal is to compromise security by stealing, altering, or denying access to information and resources.

## Hacker Communities

- White hat hackers are ethical hackers who helps enforcing security policies of an organization without crossing any legal or ethical boundaries.
- Black hat hackers are criminal hackers who exploit vulnerabilities for personal gain or malicious intent.
- Grey hat hacker who may break rules or exploit systems without permission but not with malicious intent.
- Blue hat hackers are External security testers invited by organizations to identify vulnerabilities before release.
- Green hat hacker are Inexperienced or novice hackers eager to learn and experiment, often without full awareness of consequences.
- Red hat hacker are vigilante hackers who target and disable black hats using aggressive or offensive techniques.

## NIST CSF 2.0

NIST CSF 2.0 is a framework that provides guidance to industry, government agencies, and other organizations to manage cybersecurity risks. It offers taxonomy of high-level cybersecurity outcomes that can be used by any organization regardless of their size, sector or maturity.

![[Pasted image 20251121145425.png|300]]
### Components of NIST CF 2.0

- CSF core: is a taxonomy of high level cybersecurity outcomes.
	- Functions
	- Categories
	- Sub-categories
	![[Pasted image 20251121144955.png|600]]
- CSF organization profiles: describes current and targeted cybersecurity posture
	- It is based on CSF core's outcomes
- CSF Tiers
	- Classify cybersecurity risks management rigor
	- Provides context for risk views and processes


#### Steps for creating and using a CSF organizational profile
1. Scope the Organizational profile
2. Gather needed information
3. Create the organizational profile
4. Analyze gaps and create actions plan
5. Implement action plan and update profile
