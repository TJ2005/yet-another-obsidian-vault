
## Core Goals of Security (CIA Triad)

1. **Confidentiality**
    
    - Ensuring that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals.
    - Prevents unauthorized disclosure of data.
2. **Integrity**
    
    - Ensuring that information remains accurate, consistent, and unaltered except by authorized means.
    - Protects against unauthorized modification or corruption.
3. **Availability**
    
    - Ensuring that information and resources are accessible when needed by authorized users.
    - Prevents downtime, denial-of-service, or system failures.

---

## Extended Goals of Security

Beyond the CIA triad, many frameworks include additional goals:

- **Authenticity** → Verifies that data, users, and systems are genuine and trustworthy.
- **Accountability** → Ensures actions can be traced to responsible individuals (audit trails, logging).
- **Non-repudiation** → Prevents denial of actions, ensuring proof of origin and delivery (e.g., digital signatures).
- **Privacy** → Protects personal or sensitive data from misuse or exposure.
- **Reliability** → Ensures systems and data behave consistently under expected conditions.
