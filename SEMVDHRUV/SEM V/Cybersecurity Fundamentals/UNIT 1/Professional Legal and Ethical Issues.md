
## Legal Issues

1. **Data Protection Laws**
    - GDPR, CCPA, IT Act
    - Requirements: consent, transparency, breach notification
2. **Intellectual Property**
    - Protection of software, digital assets, proprietary data
3. **Cybercrime Legislation**
    - Covers hacking, phishing, identity theft, ransomware
    - Criminal liability for unauthorized access
4. **Contractual Obligations**
    - Service-level agreements (SLAs)
    - Vendor and third-party compliance
5. **Regulatory Compliance**
    - HIPAA, PCI-DSS, ISO/IEC 27001

## Ethical Issues

1. **Privacy and Confidentiality**
    - Respecting user data beyond legal minimums
2. **Responsible Disclosure**
    - Reporting vulnerabilities ethically
3. **AI and Automation**
    - Addressing bias, accountability, unintended consequences
4. **Workplace Integrity**
    - Avoiding misuse of privileged access
5. **Cybersecurity Research Ethics**
    - Pen-testing boundaries, consent, scope
6. **Digital Divide and Equity**
    - Ensuring fair access to secure systems

## Professional Responsibilities

1. **Due Care and Due Diligence**
    - Implementing reasonable safeguards
2. **Continuous Learning**
    - Staying updated with evolving threats and laws
3. **Transparency**
    - Clear communication with stakeholders
4. **Accountability**
    - Accepting responsibility for decisions and outcomes
5. **Whistleblowing**
    - Ethical duty to report malpractice

## Common Ethical Dilemmas

1. Balancing national security vs. individual privacy
2. Deciding whether to pay ransomware demands
3. Employee monitoring vs. trust
4. Using offensive cyber tactics in defense
