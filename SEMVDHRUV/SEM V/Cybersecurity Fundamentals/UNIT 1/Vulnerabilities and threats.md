# Threats

The threat is essentially something that can harm you or your organization provided that someone gives an opportunity.

A threat on its own can't harm the or violate security policies of an organization.

***Malicious threat*** is any deliberate action that intends to damage, steal or disrupt information systems

## Types of threats

1. Interception
2. Interruption
3. Modification
4. Fabrication

### Interception
Interception is when an attacker is can gain access to an asset by intercepting the process between two authorized parties. This process can include data in transit via wireless mediums like using WIFI or Bluetooth or it can be through wired mediums like ethernet connections. 

### Interruption
Interruption is when an attacker can halt the services like access to assets by making them unavailable, unusable or completely deleting them off the records. Interruption can cause temporary or permanent damage to an organization

### Modification
The threat of modification is when an unauthorized party can change the state of an asset in an unauthorized way. 

### Fabrication
Fabrication is when an authorized party counterfeits an asset of an organization.


# Vulnerabilities

Vulnerabilities are the weaknesses that allow the threat to exploit it. 

## Types of Vulnerabilities
1. Technical: Flaws in hardware, software, or protocol that an attacker can exploit
2. Personnel: Human related weaknesses like lack of training, negligence, or insider threats
3. Physical: Inadequate protection of physical assets like server, devices or facilities
4. Operational: Weaknesses in policies, procedures, or in processes that can compromise security
