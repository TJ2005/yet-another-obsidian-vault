### Tradeoffs in CIA Triad

Need to balance CIA

1. C vs I+A
	e.g. Disconnecting Computer from the network to increase Confidentiality
	but Availability suffers and Integrity is lost due to lack of updates
2. I vs C+A
	e.g. Having extensive data checks by different users and systems to increase Integrity
	but Confidentiality suffers as more users have access to the information, Availability suffers as due to locks on data during the verification process.
