The cyber kill chain framework is a part of the intelligence driven defense model for identification and prevention of cyber intrusion activity.

## Advanced Persistent Threat

Advanced: Targeted, Coordinated, Purposeful
Persistent: Targeted, Coordinated, Purposeful
Threat: Person(s) with Intent, Opportunity, Capability

## Steps of Cyber Kill chain
1. Reconnaissance: Gathering and Mapping Information about the target organization before launching the attack.
2. Weaponization: Is a stage where we create or pack the payload that can exploit identified vulnerabilities from the previous stage
3. Delivery: Delivering the payload weaponized to the target via email, web, USB, etc.
4. Exploitation: In this phase, Attackers execute payload to exploit the vulnerability
5. Installation: Installing malware on the asset
6. Command and Control: Command channel for remote manipulation of
7. Actions on Objectives: Attackers accomplish their original goals
