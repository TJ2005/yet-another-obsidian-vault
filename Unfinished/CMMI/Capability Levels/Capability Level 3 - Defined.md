# Capability Level 3: Defined

## Overview
The process is well-characterized, understood, and conforms to organizational standards.

## Key Characteristics

- A defined process is managed and meets the organization's set of guidelines and standards
- Focus is process standardization

## Standardization
At this level, processes are defined as standards within the organization. There is a consistent approach across the organization, with documented procedures and guidelines. The emphasis is on having a standardized, well-understood process that can be followed reliably across different projects and teams.
