# Capability Level 2: Managed

## Overview
The process is planned, monitored, and controlled to ensure objectives are achieved.

## Key Characteristics

- Process is planned, monitored and controlled
- Managing the process by ensuring that objectives are achieved
- Objectives are both model and other including cost, quality, schedule
- Actively managing processing with the help of metrics

## Management Focus
At this level, the organization has established discipline in managing processes. There is planning, monitoring, and control mechanisms in place. Metrics are used to track and manage process performance, ensuring that objectives related to cost, quality, and schedule are met.
