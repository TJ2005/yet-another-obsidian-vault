# Capability Level 0: Incomplete

## Overview
This represents the lowest capability level where processes are partially or not performed.

## Key Characteristics

- **Incomplete process** - partially or not performed
- One or more specific goals of process area are not met
- No generic goals are specified for this level
- This capability level is same as maturity level 1

## Status
At this level, the organization has not successfully implemented the process or is not meeting the specific goals required for the process area.
