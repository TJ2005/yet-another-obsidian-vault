# Capability Level 5: Optimizing

## Overview
The process focuses on continuous improvement through incremental and innovative changes.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on continually improving process performance
- Performance is improved in both ways - incremental and innovation
- Emphasizes on studying the performance results across the organization to ensure that common causes or issues are identified and fixed

## Continuous Improvement
At this level, the organization is focused on continuous process improvement. Improvements are made both incrementally (small, continuous enhancements) and through innovation (breakthrough improvements). The organization analyzes performance data across projects and teams to identify common issues and systemic problems, implementing improvements that benefit the entire organization.
