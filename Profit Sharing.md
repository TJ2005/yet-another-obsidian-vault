## Partnership and Profit Sharing: Advanced Formulas

In competitive exams and business mathematics, the distribution of profit depends on two primary factors: **Investment ($I$)** and **Time ($T$)**.

---

### 1. General Profit Sharing Ratio

When multiple partners invest different amounts for different periods of time, the profit ($P$) is shared in the ratio of the product of their investment and time.

$$P_1 : P_2 : P_3 = (I_1 \times T_1) : (I_2 \times T_2) : (I_3 \times T_3)$$

---

### 2. Two-Partner Comparison

To compare the profit of two specific partners directly:

$$\frac{P_1}{P_2} = \frac{I_1 \times T_1}{I_2 \times T_2}$$

---

### 3. Derived Formulas (Finding Unknowns)

If you know the Profit Ratio and one of the other variables, you can rearrange the formula to find the missing Investment or Time:

#### To find the Investment Ratio:

If you know the profit shares and the time periods:


$$\frac{I_1}{I_2} = \frac{P_1}{T_1} \div \frac{P_2}{T_2} \implies I_1 : I_2 = \frac{P_1}{T_1} : \frac{P_2}{T_2}$$

#### To find the Time Ratio:

If you know the profit shares and the investment amounts:


$$\frac{T_1}{T_2} = \frac{P_1}{I_1} \div \frac{P_2}{I_2} \implies T_1 : T_2 = \frac{P_1}{I_1} : \frac{P_2}{I_2}$$

---

### 4. Working vs. Sleeping Partners

In some scenarios, a "Working Partner" takes a percentage of the profit as a salary *before* the remaining profit is split.

1. **Total Profit** = $P_{total}$
2. **Management Commission/Salary** = $S$
3. **Distributable Profit** = $P_{total} - S$
4. **Final Share** = $(\text{Ratio Share of } P_{distributable}) + \text{Salary (if applicable)}$

---

### Quick Reference Table

| Goal | Formula / Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| **Profit Ratio** | $P \propto I \times T$ |
| **Investment Ratio** | $I \propto \frac{P}{T}$ |
| **Time Ratio** | $T \propto \frac{P}{I}$ |

> [!TIP]
> Always convert time units to be consistent (e.g., all in months or all in years) before calculating the ratio.
